From a Student Problem to an AI Solution

My journey exploring Generative AI and the idea behind Padhai Dost — an AI-powered study companion for students.

Md Asmu Rahmani · Darbhanga, Bihar, India

I have always been interested in technology, programming, and Artificial Intelligence. As a Computer Science student, I spend a lot of time learning new concepts, solving programming problems, and exploring how technology can make everyday tasks easier.

My Journey as a Builder

The Gen AI Academy APAC – Meet The Builders initiative gave me an opportunity to think beyond simply learning about AI. It encouraged me to think like a builder.

Instead of asking only, “What can AI do?” I started asking:

“What real problem can I solve with AI?”

That question became the starting point of my project idea.

The Problem

Students have access to an enormous amount of educational content today. However, having information is not the same as understanding it.

A student may have difficulty understanding a programming concept, preparing revision notes, finding practice questions, or deciding what to study next.

Searching through multiple websites and resources can also become time-consuming.

I experienced this challenge myself while studying technical subjects. This made me think about creating an AI-based study companion that could provide simple, conversational and personalized academic assistance.

My Idea: Padhai Dost

My project idea is Padhai Dost, an AI-powered study assistant for students.

The idea is simple: A student asks a question in normal language, and the AI helps explain the concept in a clear and structured way.

Simple concept explanations
Programming & technical help
Revision notes
Practice questions
Topic-wise learning
Conversational doubt solving
Hinglish-friendly explanations
Personalized study suggestions

The goal is not to replace teachers, books, or educational institutions. Instead, Padhai Dost is intended to act as an additional learning companion that students can use whenever they need help understanding a topic.

Why Generative AI?

Traditional software generally follows predefined instructions. Generative AI makes it possible to interact with technology more naturally.

“Mujhe Priority Scheduling simple example ke saath samjhao.”

A student can communicate naturally instead of having to search for the exact wording of a question. This conversational experience is one of the reasons I find Generative AI particularly interesting.

My Building Approach

I am approaching the project in small steps. First, I am focusing on the core student experience: asking a question and receiving a useful answer.

Then I plan to improve the system with better prompting, structured responses, practice questions, and learning-focused features.

I also want to explore how cloud and Generative AI tools can be used to turn the idea into a practical application.

The important lesson for me is that building an AI application is not simply about connecting an AI model to a user interface.

  • A clearly defined problem
  • Good user experience
  • Careful prompt design
  • Testing
  • Reliable outputs
  • Responsible use of AI
  • Continuous improvement

What I Am Learning

This journey is teaching me that you do not need to know everything before you start building.

As a student, I am still learning programming, AI, cloud technologies, and software development.

But building something forces me to learn these concepts practically. Whenever I face a technical problem, I have to research it, understand it, experiment with it, and try again.

That process is helping me move from being only a learner to becoming a builder.

Future Vision

I want to continue improving Padhai Dost with features such as:

  • PDF-based learning
  • Chapter-wise revision
  • Personalized study plans
  • Automatic quizzes
  • Progress tracking
  • Voice interaction
  • Multilingual learning
  • Better source-based answers
  • Personalized recommendations

My Biggest Takeaway

The biggest lesson I have learned from this experience is simple:

Start with a problem, not with technology.

Generative AI is powerful, but the technology itself is not the final goal. The real value comes from using that technology to solve a problem that people actually experience.

As a student from Darbhanga, Bihar, I want to continue exploring how AI can be used to solve practical problems around me.

Conclusion

The Meet The Builders journey is not just about creating one project. It is about developing the mindset to observe problems, build solutions, learn from failures, and keep improving.

And this is only the beginning of my journey as a builder.
